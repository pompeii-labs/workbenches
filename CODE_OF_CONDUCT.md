# Code of Conduct

Workbench contributors must keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and
focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and implementations without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction in good faith.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and coordinated security disclosure.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information
are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@pompeiilabs.com`. Maintainers will
review reports confidentially to the extent practical and may remove content,
restrict participation, or revoke project access. Anyone directly involved in
a report will not decide its outcome when another maintainer is available.

This policy applies to repository activity and project-related public or
private communication.
